## Critical & Warning Issues

### 🔴 CRITICAL

- None.

### ⚠️ WARNING

1. **`anytype-mongodb` backup had been failing since 2026-10-26 02:00.** The last good snapshot was 2026-10-25. Volsync wrote six failed mover pods in ten minutes, all with `chtimes /cache/...: operation not permitted`. **Cause:** the cache PVC is 126 days old and was first filled by the root mover, before `moverSecurityContext` uid/gid 999 was added (2026-06-23, `0d60ab0d`). Volsync 0.17.0 (merged 2026-10-25, `4094d4e1`) bundles restic 0.19.1, which now calls `chtimes` on existing cache files, and uid 999 does not own them. **Fixed:** deleted the mover job and `volsync-src-anytype-mongodb-cache`; volsync recreated both and the backup succeeded (snapshot `718c59eb`, 908 MiB). Not detected by any alert.
2. **`nextcloud-occ-preview-pre-generate` failed once** (2026-10-26 10:50, `Unable to save a preview because it already exists but fetching it again yields nothing`) and the job stayed for 34 hours because `backoffLimit` was 0 and `failedJobsHistoryLimit` is 3. Every later run (every 10 minutes) succeeded. Looks like a race with another preview write.
3. **`talos-backup` job failed on 2026-10-24 at 15:00**, S3 on the QNAP returned HTTP 503 for `PutObject` for about two hours (six retries). Later backups succeed, but `failedJobsHistoryLimit: 1` kept the failed job, so `KubeJobFailed` stayed up for three days.
